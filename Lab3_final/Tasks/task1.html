4 Combinators

descendant: div p → blue

Descendant paragraph inside div – blue

Nested paragraph – also blue (still descendant)

child: div > h4 → red

Direct child h4 – red

Nested h4 – NOT red (not direct child)

adjacent: h4 + p → yellow bg
general sibling: h4 ~ span → green bold

Heading

Adjacent sibling p – yellow background

Second p – no yellow

General sibling span – green bold

More text

Another span – also green bold

5 Pseudo-classes

  • Item 1 – :first-child (blue bold)
  • Item 2 – :nth-child(2) (green bg)
  • Item 3 – hover me (:hover)
  • Item 4 – hover me
  • Item 5 – :last-child (red bold)
