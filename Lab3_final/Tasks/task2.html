Font Sizes – Different Units

12px – Very small (captions)

14px – Small body text

16px – Normal body text

20px – Medium heading

1.5em – Relative to parent

1.5rem – Relative to root (16px)

130% – Percentage of parent font size

Text Alignment

text-align: left – default, starts from left edge

text-align: center – centred on the line

text-align: right – pushed to the right edge

text-align: justify – stretched to fill the full line width. Both edges are flush, just like a newspaper column or book paragraph layout that you see in print.
