line-height: 1.8 – Comfortable spacing. This paragraph has more space between each line which makes it much easier to read especially for longer blocks of text on screen.


line-height: 2.5 – Very loose spacing. This paragraph has a lot of white space between lines. It feels very airy and spacious.
