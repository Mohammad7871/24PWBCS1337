Box-sizing: content-box vs border-box

box-sizing

Both boxes have width: 280px and padding: 20px and border: 3px. With content-box the actual rendered width is wider. With border-box the total stays exactly 280px.

content-box – width: 280px but rendered wider because padding and border are ADDED on top.
border-box – width: 280px and that is the TOTAL including padding and border. Same width as above set in CSS.
