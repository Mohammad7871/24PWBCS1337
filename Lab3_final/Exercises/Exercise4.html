Exercise 4 – CSS Combinators

Descendant, Child, Adjacent Sibling, General Sibling

Descendant Combinator – div p

space between selectors

This paragraph is inside a div – it gets blue colour (div p selector).

This is a span inside the div (not matched by div p).

This nested paragraph also matches (it is still a descendant of div).

The descendant combinator selects ALL matching descendants, not just direct children.

Child Combinator – div > h3

> between selectors

This h3 is a DIRECT child of div – gets red colour.

This h3 is nested inside another div – NOT a direct child, no red colour.

Adjacent Sibling – h2 + p

+ between selectors

This is an h2 heading

This paragraph comes right after h2 – it gets a yellow background.

This second paragraph does NOT get yellow (it is not adjacent to h2).

General Sibling – h2 ~ span

~ between selectors

This is an h2 heading

Paragraph (not a span, not affected)

This span comes after h2 – green and bold (h2 ~ span).

Another paragraph

This span also gets green and bold (still a sibling of h2).
