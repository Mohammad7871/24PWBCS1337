Heading with Stars

The red * symbols were added by CSS using h2::before and h2::after – no HTML was changed.

::first-letter – Large Drop Cap

::first-letter

his paragraph demonstrates the first-letter pseudo-element. The very first letter is made large and blue, just like a drop cap in a book or magazine. The rest of the paragraph continues in normal size. Hover over the paragraph to see the first letter turn red.

::first-line – Bold First Line

::first-line

This is the first line of the paragraph. It will be displayed in bold because of the ::first-line pseudo-element. The second line and the rest of the paragraph will appear in normal weight without any bold styling applied to them.

::selection – Custom Highlight Colour

::selection

Try selecting / highlighting any text on this page with your mouse. The selection background will turn yellow instead of the default blue because of the ::selection pseudo-element.
