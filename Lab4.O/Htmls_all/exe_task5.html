Preference Media Queries – Simple Demo

1. Dark Mode (prefers-color-scheme: dark)

Ye page apne aap dark mode mein badal jata hai agar aapka system/computer dark mode pe set ho.

Example Area

Change your phone/computer to dark mode and refresh the page → colors will change automatically.

2. Reduced Motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)

Agar aapke system mein "reduce motion" on hai, to neeche wali animation nahi chalegi ya bohot slow ho jayegi.

Important for: People who feel uncomfortable or dizzy because of moving elements.

3. Print Styles (@media print)

Press Ctrl + P (or Cmd + P on Mac) to see print preview.

You should see:

  • White background + black text
  • No blue buttons or colors
  • Larger text for reading
